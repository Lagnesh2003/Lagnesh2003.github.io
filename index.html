Hi, I’m Lagnesh!

I work on robot learning: reinforcement learning and vision-language-action models that control real robots. I build the whole stack myself, from MuJoCo environments and training code to the robots on my desk: a pair of SO-101 arms for imitation learning and a Furuta pendulum I designed for learning on hardware.

I come to this from control. I spent a year as a GNC engineer at Skyroot Aerospace, working on reaction control for the Vikram-1 launch vehicle and attitude control for CubeSats, and I now work on perception and autonomy at LAT Aerospace. That background shapes how I do learning: every policy is compared against a classical controller, every result is reported over seeds with confidence intervals, and I write down what did not work.

I graduated from IIT Bombay in 2025 with a CPI of 9.36, rank 2 in my department. I’m looking for robot learning roles in RL and VLA. Email me at lagnesh22@gmail.com.

Research interests

  • Sample-efficient RL on real hardware. Robots should learn hard skills from minutes of real data without breaking themselves. I compare PPO and SAC against LQR and energy-based control in GPU simulation, and test them on a pendulum I am building. Key work: saferl-lab, Furuta pendulum rig
  • Imitation learning and VLAs on low-cost arms. What does it take for a policy trained on a few hundred demonstrations to work on a cheap arm? I study it end to end on the SO-101: task, expert, data, training, and evaluation that says how much of a result is noise. Key work: SO-101 pipeline, ACT ablations
  • World models. Learned dynamics that stay accurate over long rollouts and know when they are wrong, the base for model-based RL and safety filters. Key work: Furuta world models, uncertainty
